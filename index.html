Challenges & Engineering Solutions

01 · High Implementation Cost SOLVED

Sensors, edge compute, and connected infrastructure require capital.

Solution: Leverage existing smartphone camera networks + crowd-sourced accelerometer telemetry to lower barrier.
02 · Data Privacy Concerns SOLVED

Continuous monitoring of drivers and pedestrians demands strict governance.

Solution: On-device edge processing only — no raw video leaves the vehicle; only anonymous facial vectors computed locally.
03 · Connectivity Dependence SOLVED

Real-time decisions need reliable, low-latency networks across all conditions.

Solution: Local fail-safe autonomy: AEB and DMS operate 100% offline without requiring internet or cellular connectivity.
04 · Accuracy & Reliability SOLVED

Models must perform safely across rain, fog, nighttime glare, and unusual postures.

Solution: Multi-modal sensor fusion combines camera, radar, and LiDAR with transformer confidence thresholds.
